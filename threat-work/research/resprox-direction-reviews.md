## Quality bar for every review

Judge useful engineering and research distinctiveness separately. A known technique can remain a practical baseline without satisfying the novelty objective. For each leading idea require: a precise mechanism; an obtainable discriminating observation; the closest simpler/known approach; the decision that approach misses; the strongest benign explanation; and one experiment with explicit rejection conditions. A different name, extra join, longer specification or additional citation is not a contribution by itself.

Keep at most three active breakthrough candidates, with room for a genuinely different challenger. Do not invent a third to fill the list. Missing telemetry parks an implementation branch, not necessarily the underlying mechanism. A benign twin defeats unique attribution, but only a fair comparison can determine whether the feature adds investigative value. Report designed, executed-synthetic, sensor/lab and field validation separately; designer-chosen fixtures do not establish field precision or recall.
